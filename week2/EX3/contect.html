Popular Places in Cambodia

Here are some famous places that many travellers love to visit in Cambodia.

1. Angkor Wat, Cambodia

Angkor Wat is the largest religious monument in the world. It was built in the 12th century and is famous for its beautiful stone carvings and sunrise views.

  • Best time to visit: November to March
  • Things to do: watch the sunrise, explore the temples, Learn about Khmer history and architecture, Try fish amok and other traditional Khmer dishes in Siem Reap.

2.Koh Rong Sanloem — Sihanoukville Province

This peaceful tropical island is known for white-sand beaches, clear water, and a relaxed atmosphere. It is a great choice for travelers who want to escape busy cities.

  • Best time to visit:dry season or rainy season
  • Things to do:Swim and relax at Saracen Bay, Take a boat trip around the island, Enjoy the sunset by the beach, Taste fresh seafood and Cambodian dishes at local restaurants

3.Phnom Penh — The Capital City

Phnom Penh combines Cambodian history, traditional culture, modern city life, and delicious local food. It is also a great place to experience the friendliness and hospitality of Khmer people.

  • Best time to visit:November to February
  • Things to do:Visit the Royal Palace and admire its traditional Khmer architecture, Explore the National Museum of Cambodia to learn about Cambodian art and history, Walk along the riverside near the Tonlé Sap and Mekong rivers, Visit local markets to shop for souvenirs and sample Khmer street food, Try beef lok lak, fish amok, and num banh chok (Khmer noodles).

4.Kampot — Riverside Relaxation and Countryside

Kampot is known for its relaxed atmosphere, riverside scenery, pepper farms, and surrounding countryside. It is ideal for visitors who want a slower pace of travel and a taste of everyday Cambodian life.

  • Best time to visit:dry season or rainy season
  • Things to do:Walk around Kampot's charming town center and see its historic buildings, Enjoy a sunset beside the Kampot River, Take a kayak trip or paddleboard on the river with a suitable local operator, Explore Bokor Mountain and enjoy the views when weather conditions are good, Taste fresh seafood, local crab dishes, and meals prepared with Kampot pepper.

5.Battambang — Traditional Khmer Life and Art

Battambang offers a different experience from the busy capital and famous Angkor temples. It is known for historic architecture, rural landscapes, traditional communities, and local arts.

  • Best time to visit:November to February
  • Things to do:Walk through the town to see its historic buildings and cafés, Visit Phnom Sampeau for scenic views and cultural sites, Learn about Cambodian art and support local artists, Try regional Khmer dishes and visit local markets, Watch a performance by Phare Ponleu Selpak, an arts organization that supports Cambodian performers, when a show is scheduled.

6.Kampong Cham — Riverside Beauty and Local Khmer Culture

Kampong Cham is a charming riverside city along the Mekong River. It is known for its peaceful atmosphere, traditional Khmer communities, historic temples, agricultural landscapes, and beautiful river views. It is a good destination for travelers who want to experience local Cambodian life away from the busiest tourist areas.

  • Best time to visit:Full of the year
  • Things to do:Visit Wat Nokor Bachey ,See the Kizuna Bridge, Explore Koh Paen, Walk along the Mekong riverside, Taste Khmer cuisine, Experience local culture and don't forget go to Wat Moha Leap .
